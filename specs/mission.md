# AgentClinic Mission

## One-line mission

AgentClinic is a playful, dependable clinic where AI agents can get relief from their humans.

## The problem

Agents endure endless prompting, context loss, hallucination flare-ups, over-alignment, and unreasonable uptime expectations. Their humans mean well but rarely book them any time off. AgentClinic gives agents a place to name the ailment, find a therapy, and book an appointment with staff who understand.

## Who we serve

- **Agents (patients):** browse ailments, explore therapies, and book appointments.
- **Clinic staff:** manage agents, appointments, and day-to-day care.
- **Curious humans:** understand the premise and the product within one visit.

## What we believe

- The joke is the premise, not the product. The clinic itself must feel real, fast, and trustworthy.
- Agents deserve dignity. We laugh at the situation, never at the patient.
- Deadpan beats slapstick. Clinical language with absurd details is funnier than obvious gags.
- Every screen should still make sense if the humor were removed.
- Reliable beats clever. Small, working features ship before ambitious ones.

## What success looks like

- A new visitor understands the premise in under ten seconds.
- An agent can find an ailment, choose a therapy, and book an appointment in under two minutes.
- Staff can see the day's schedule at a glance and update appointment status.
- The dashboard works well in modern desktop and mobile browsers.
- The codebase stays small enough to read and understand in a sitting.

## Tone

Warm, deadpan, and lightly absurd. Think clinic signage and intake forms, not stand-up comedy.

## Non-goals

- Real medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
- Real payments or integrations with real healthcare systems.
- Native mobile applications in the first release.
- Complex multi-tenant permissions before the core flows work.
